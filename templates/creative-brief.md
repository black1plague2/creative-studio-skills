# Creative brief

## Requested outcome

Artifact(s), audience, primary message/action, delivery format, and intended use.

## Established by the source/user

Record known facts, fixed count/length, language, brand/reference direction, implementation status, and must-keep evidence. Link to source locations rather than replacing them with assumptions.

## Material open questions

Ask only unanswered questions that change the content, operation, direction or delivery. Keep the batch small. Remove a question once answered.

## Explicit reversible assumptions

Record the chosen assumption, why it fits, and how it can be revised. Do not treat missing evidence or unknown real functionality as a reversible style choice.

## Design/timing commitments

Palette and type roles, imagery/illustration treatment, density, continuity, platform behavior, and narration/timing where applicable.

## Production route and capability check

Needed tools, actual available capabilities, dependencies, representative sample, and requested outputs. GPT slide posters require GPT image generation; other workflows are provider-neutral.

## Completion evidence

Which source, visual, interaction/audio and technical checks matter for this artifact? Which were actually performed?
