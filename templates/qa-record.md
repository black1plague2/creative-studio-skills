# Artifact review record

Record artifact/version, date, reviewer, rendering/playback environment, and scope.

| Severity | Page/state/timecode | Observed defect | Audience impact | Fix | Actual recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Source fidelity

Claims, exact text, numbers, units, caveats and diagram relationships checked against source.

## Craft / interaction / audio

Rendered evidence, relevant states, playback/listening observations, and any subjective design judgments.

## Delivery integrity

Actual output opening/rendering, geometry, count/order, format, and dependencies checked.

## Unrun checks and remaining work

State what did not run and why. A visual score, manifest flag or waveform cannot replace the relevant inspection.
