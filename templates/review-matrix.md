# Cross-artifact review matrix

Use this record to separate observable checks. A filled source ledger does not prove a claim, a manifest flag does not prove spelling, and a passing build does not prove the interface works.

| Pass | Question | Evidence location | Actual result | Unrun / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source fidelity | Are claims, names, units, caveats and relationships faithful? | Source/page/row/claim ID | Record verified and supplied-only status | Identify missing evidence |
| Audience/message | Does the artifact support the intended understanding/action? | Narrative, key page/scene/task | Record what leads and what is confusing | Identify audience assumptions |
| Visual craft | Does hierarchy, imagery, typography and continuity serve this topic? | Rendered page/screenshot/frame | Specific observations and defects | State any unrendered output |
| Interaction/accessibility/audio | Can the audience operate/read/listen in the intended environment? | State, focus path, device, audition/timecode | Actual operations/listening checks | Distinguish emulation from real device and unrun listening |
| Technical delivery | Does the final file/export open with correct format/order? | Renderer/exporter/file/codec check | Actual count/order/proportions/open result | State export substitutions/dependencies |

## Defect and repair log

| Severity | Page/state/timecode | Observed defect | Audience impact | Proposed fix | Actual recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classify blockers, major issues and minor polish proportionally to the requested use. Do not average away a blocker. If the renderer substitutes a font, record the environment and inspect the actual wrapping. For documents edited in place, compare affected regions before/after. For a video, inspect full playback and listen; a contact sheet and metadata cover only part of that task.

## Final status

State one: complete artifact, reviewed draft, local prototype, source/planning handoff, or blocked production step. Identify the requested outputs actually delivered and the checks actually performed. Do not label example specifications, silent motion studies or model presets as complete narrated/validated creative work.
