# Research registry

[sources.csv](sources.csv) contains the 58-repository survey that informed the library. It separates skills, engines, creator examples and discovery directories, and records inspected material, useful audit structures, limitations, fit decisions and license observations.

The survey is a bounded source inspection, not a benchmark or an endorsement of every implementation. No upstream implementation is automatically installed. References can move or change; verify the specific primary file and terms before importing code/assets or using model/API-specific details.

The skills in this repository are original synthesis guided by that research and the user's preference for complete illustrated slide posters. Public source availability alone is not proof of human authorship, professional output, accurate claims, or unrestricted reuse.
