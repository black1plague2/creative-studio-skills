# Public interpretation of the visual preferences

This original text-only note replaces the local reference-brief link in the archived report. It records broad creative preferences without publishing the supplied decks/video or their screenshots, private claims or personal content.

## Presentation contract

Generate a complete, deliberately designed image per slide using GPT image generation. Use a coherent, topic-specific illustration, palette, typography, motif and diagram grammar. Package approved 16:9 images into PDF and image-based PPTX when requested. Keep exact-copy, source and prompt records. An editable-object deck or deterministic text overlay is an explicit alternate/exception, not the default.

## Range of visual treatment

The preferences support a playful sketchbook style with orderly technical labels, culturally grounded editorial ornament, richly illustrated environmental/campus worlds, and restrained illustration with strong type. They include both open narrative compositions and dense technical infographics. Choose the direction for the content; do not copy one color palette, person, motif or layout into every topic.

Architecture and user flows are legitimate when visually authored around actual actors, components, branches and consequences. Generic text boxes and arrows are not a substitute for art direction. Conversely, dense illustration is not permission to make labels unreadable or change the evidence.

## Video contract

Use concise spoken copy, progressive visual demonstrations, stable object/character/device identity, concrete product screens where real, readable captions and measured narration timing. Do not reduce the film to pans over dense slides. Viewer activity needs a web/learning layer only when actual choices, hotspots or branches are requested.

Natural narration requires an audition and direct listening. A waveform, transcript, preset name or animation screenshot cannot establish the perceived voice quality. Missing listening capability remains an explicit outstanding check.

These preferences shape defaults but preserve the live user's audience, brand, reference, language and delivery requirements. Use the relevant skill and domain pack for a new task.
