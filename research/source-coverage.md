## What incorporation means

Every catalog entry is accounted for. Workflow references inform task-specific rules and audit structures; tools/engines inform an optional route, not a dependency automatically installed everywhere; creator material informs a technique without copying its restricted assets; a discovery catalog remains a way to find primary sources rather than proof of quality. The JSON contains the original useful findings, inspection depth, license observations and specific integration files for each source.

The [complete report](full-research-report.md), [registry](sources.csv), [domain packs](../domains/README.md) and [artifact templates](../templates/README.md) give the detailed production context. All domains and creative types remain extensible; a finite set of packs is not a claim to exhaust every industry or future tool.
