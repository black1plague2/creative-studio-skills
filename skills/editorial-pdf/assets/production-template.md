## Filled example — FernStay concept booklet

Domain: [hospitality](../../../domains/hospitality.md). Source: fictional guesthouse concept; no operating property, rates, address or verified facilities. Audience: adult travelers evaluating a visual concept. Output: three-page screen-reading A4 PDF with editable authoring source, not a booking document.

| Page | Content | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | “A quieter weekend, imagined.” and concept status | Large courtyard illustration with clear “Illustrative concept; not an operating property.” caption |
| 2 | Courtyard, reading nook and room entrance | Three labeled original renderings; no invented facilities/access statements |
| 3 | Concept scope and open production questions | Readable body copy; explain real availability/rates/access details are unspecified |

Direction: cream ground, deep green headings, warm serif display and highly legible body font available to the renderer; no fragile font assumptions. Image captions remain semantic text. Avoid shrinking page-three limitations to make room for art. No contact/booking action is invented.
