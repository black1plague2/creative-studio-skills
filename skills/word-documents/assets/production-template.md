## Filled example — Mira Rao case-study memo

Domain: [personal and professional](../../../domains/personal-professional.md). This is a fictional person/exercise, not a real resume. Source supplies flow mapping and three prototype states; product is neither shipped nor tested. No credentials, dates, employer or quantified result exists.

Output: editable two-page DOCX case-study memo. Title “A clearer appointment request.” Heading 1 sections: “Problem hypothesis”, “Contribution”, “Prototype states”, “What remains untested”. Contribution copy: “Flow mapping and prototype states.” Qualification: “Fictional case study; not a shipped product.” States table: choosing slot, received request and failed submission. Preserve request-versus-confirmation distinction. No invented email or degree is added.

Layout: one-column readable body; native headings and a table with clear repeated header where needed; captions link illustrations to the prototype's status. A “Reduced errors by 40%” phrase would be rejected for missing evidence, not rewritten as a softer success claim.

## Render and handoff

For edits, compare affected pages before/after and inspect wider pagination if content moves. For new documents, render all pages. Check style inheritance, list numbering, table wrap/splitting, font substitution, orphan headings, footers and exact content. Reopen the DOCX in a compatible viewer when possible. Deliver DOCX plus requested PDF/source notes.

Record: source/content check [evidence]; render [actual pages or not run]; openability [actual result]; final file [path]; remaining limits [fill]. Example above is a filled plan, not a generated or visually checked document.
