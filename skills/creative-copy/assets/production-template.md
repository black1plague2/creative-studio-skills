## Filled example — PocketPlan budgeting sandbox

Domain: [fintech](../../../domains/fintech.md). Synthetic monthly example in sample units, not a real account or recommendation. Income 2,000; essentials 1,100; flexible 500; unallocated 400. No bank connection or savings claim.

| Role | Draft wording | Evidence/decision |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | “See where your sample budget goes.” | Truthfully names sample context |
| Supporting line | “Change a category and see what remains unallocated.” | Proposed sandbox behavior; does not guarantee better finances |
| Figure caption | “Synthetic monthly example; not a recommendation.” | Keeps status next to amounts |
| Invalid input | “Enter a number to continue.” | Clear recovery, no blame |
| Over-allocation | “Your categories exceed the sample income. Adjust an amount.” | Specific consequence and next step |

Rejected variant: “Save 400 every month.” The value is an example residual, not measured savings. A narration version says “four hundred sample units remain unallocated” and preserves the synthetic qualification; actual pronunciation/timing review waits for audio.

## Acceptance record

Check every factual implication against source, including adjectives such as guaranteed, verified, available and proven. Read copy in the real layout/interaction or aloud for narration; word count alone does not prove fit. Record final selected wording, source trace, context render/listening evidence where applicable and actual unresolved facts. The example is a copy draft; no rendered or listened acceptance is claimed.
