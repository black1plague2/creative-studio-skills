# GPT poster slides: acceptance and repair

## Text-only GPT handoff acceptance

When a handoff is requested or GPT image generation is unavailable, check the resolved brief, source ledger, slide count/order, visual-system/reference roles, exact-copy record, one complete prompt per slide, output requirements and pending review plan. Mark draft-manifest entries unreviewed. Do not require generated images for this mode, and do not claim image/export checks passed. The packet must be sufficient for GPT to generate the intended posters without inventing missing factual content.

## Generated-image acceptance

- Every final page is a complete GPT-generated poster image, or a explicitly authorized exception is recorded.
- The canvas is 16:9; no stretched artwork, unintended crop, or missing content.
- All source claims, exact text, units, caveats, and diagram relationships are checked.
- The illustrated identity remains coherent across cover, evidence, architecture, flow, and closing.
- Contact sheet and individual full-size images were inspected.
- PDF/PPTX order and page count match the manifest and are reopened for final review.

## Finding format

Record severity, location/page/state/timecode, observed defect, audience impact, proposed fix, and recheck result. Separate a confirmed defect, a taste judgment, and a check that could not run.

- **Blocker:** incorrect essential evidence, missing output, broken primary action, unreadable core content, or an invalid final file.
- **Major:** a serious clarity, continuity, layout or recovery problem that needs correction before use.
- **Minor:** a local polish issue that does not prevent understanding or operation.

Fix blockers before adding decoration. Recheck changed output and the wider artifact only when the change affects it. Do not invent a universal numerical score or use a passing mechanical check to conceal an unreviewed visual/audio result.
