# Brand identity: production rules

These rules apply to the selected task and supplied brief. Use judgment for reversible choices; preserve explicit user requirements.

## 1. Ground identity in the subject and audience

Ground identity in the subject and audience. Describe concrete visual/verbal traits: scholarly and evidence-forward, campus-community and illustrated, or industrial and precise. Vague adjectives alone are not a direction.

## 2. Use references as evidence for specific roles rather than averaging their appearances

Use references as evidence for specific roles rather than averaging their appearances. One may inform type hierarchy, another illustration texture; establish a dominant coherent direction.

## 3. Show real content specimens before expanding the system: headline, body paragraph, button/control, poster region, and a useful scene

Show real content specimens before expanding the system: headline, body paragraph, button/control, poster region, and a useful scene. A palette swatch page alone does not prove the identity works.

## 4. Define palette by roles: canvas, text, supporting surface, accent, and semantic states

Define palette by roles: canvas, text, supporting surface, accent, and semantic states. A brand accent is not automatically an error color. Check contrast in the actual composition and supported themes.

## 5. Define typography by job, hierarchy and content length

Define typography by job, hierarchy and content length. Limit families for coherence, but do not impose an arbitrary worldwide font ban. Distinguish projection, editorial prose, native UI and decorative lettering.

## 6. Choose motifs and illustration grammar that can extend to content-heavy pages

Choose motifs and illustration grammar that can extend to content-heavy pages. Specify stroke, texture, character proportions, photography art direction, crops and pattern use. Do not rely on one decorative header stripe to carry the identity.

## 7. For a logo request, create original concepts and verify small-size, monochrome and dark/light use

For a logo request, create original concepts and verify small-size, monochrome and dark/light use. Supply vector/source when feasible; an AI-generated logo sketch is not a trademark clearance.

## 8. Document voice with concrete examples: headline, error, description, CTA and narration

Document voice with concrete examples: headline, error, description, CTA and narration. Preserve clear language and product truth. Avoid invented testimonials or slogans that make unsupported claims.

## 9. Create a few representative cross-media examples using the same identity

Create a few representative cross-media examples using the same identity. Adapt density and interaction conventions for each medium instead of pasting the poster treatment into app controls.

## 10. Deliver tokens/specimens/assets, permitted alternatives, font/source notes, and a brief decision ledger

Deliver tokens/specimens/assets, permitted alternatives, font/source notes, and a brief decision ledger. Preserve existing identity when the user only requested one artifact.
