# Brand identity: acceptance and repair

- Identity choices trace to the actual subject and audience.
- Type, color, imagery, motifs and copy form a coherent system.
- Real content works in small, dense and large specimens.
- Required existing marks and brand rules survive.
- Files and third-party font/asset terms are documented.

## Finding format

Record severity, location/page/state/timecode, observed defect, audience impact, proposed fix, and recheck result. Separate a confirmed defect, a taste judgment, and a check that could not run.

- **Blocker:** incorrect essential evidence, missing output, broken primary action, unreadable core content, or an invalid final file.
- **Major:** a serious clarity, continuity, layout or recovery problem that needs correction before use.
- **Minor:** a local polish issue that does not prevent understanding or operation.

Fix blockers before adding decoration. Recheck changed output and the wider artifact only when the change affects it. Do not invent a universal numerical score or use a passing mechanical check to conceal an unreviewed visual/audio result.
