## Filled example — FrameKind film concept identity

Domain: [creative portfolio/media](../../../domains/creative-media.md). Fictional “Between Trains” storyboard, not a released film. Audience: a creative reviewer. Perception: attentive, patient and cinematic; no invented award or client association.

Palette roles: midnight navy #172431 field, warm paper #EFE6D5 text panel, amber #DCA451 light emphasis. Type roles: strong readable serif display for the title and simple sans-serif credits/captions using available fonts. Motif: a consistent station frame where the light changes; no decorative camera icons merely to announce film. Verbal rule: short sentences about actual artistic choices, with “Fictional film concept and storyboard” visible where status matters.

Specimens to make and inspect: one title/keyframe composition, one portfolio case-study section and one social crop with credits/status. Preserve station/object identity across imagery. Variation belongs in the light, not arbitrary new palette/characters. A GPT deck would generate these rules as complete 16:9 posters; website/campaign production remains tool-neutral.
