## Filled example — BenchTrace inventory plate

Domain: [industrial and scientific](../../../domains/industrial-scientific.md). Fictional lab-inventory concept, no operating safety instruction or assay data. Output: one digital landscape infographic, 1600 × 1000 target where supported; it is not a requested slide deck.

Headline: “Know where the sample belongs.” Three illustrated groupings: scan a sample label → look up its record → find assigned shelf. Exact example labels: “BT-A”, “Shelf A1”, “BT-B”, “Shelf B2”. Footnote/status: “Fictional inventory concept.” A small error branch states “Unknown identifier. Check the label.” It never guesses a shelf.

Technical editorial direction: off-white field, charcoal text, blue shelf rails and restrained amber highlights. Physical sample, label, record and shelf are visually distinct but connected; exact arrows reflect lookup rather than a sample being chemically transformed. Source gives no tracking accuracy, so no results chart appears. Density supports the labels instead of shrinking them behind a giant slogan.

## Inspection record

A generated false label is regenerated/edited and rechecked, not excused as decorative text.
