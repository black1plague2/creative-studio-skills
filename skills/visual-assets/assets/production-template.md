## Filled example — VisitBridge supporting illustration

Domain: [healthtech](../../../domains/healthtech.md). Administrative scheduling concept, no clinical claim. One supporting illustration for a visitor guide: adult visitor holding a calendar card beside a neutral receipt; neither person is depicted as a medical professional. No patient identity or real medical record is included.

Filled prompt:

> Create an original warm editorial illustration of an adult visitor comparing a calendar card with a request receipt. Cream and muted teal cut-paper forms with clear ink contours, calm expression and natural hands. Landscape composition with open space at upper left for later semantic guide text; do not generate lettering inside the asset. The receipt suggests a received request, not a confirmed appointment. No medical chart, clinician endorsement, diagnosis imagery, logo or certification badge. This is a fictional administrative concept. Use a solid cream background; no transparency is needed for this placement.

Asset is tool-neutral because it supports a reading document. If used within the user's GPT presentation, the complete slide including lettering must still be generated via the GPT poster route; this separate illustration is not a silent replacement.

## Inspection and delivery record

Inspect anatomy/objects, unintended text, transparent edge quality where requested, dimensions, crop, contrast with surrounding copy and continuity. Place it in the real layout before acceptance; a beautiful standalone image may leave no room for the message. Record actual file/metadata, prompt, reference roles, rights/status and reviewed variants. Generated illustrative imagery remains labeled where viewers might mistake it for documentary evidence. Current example: prompt only, image checks unrun.
