# Worked example: Explainer video

This is an original fictional exercise, not a claim of a real deployed product or evidence that every host can execute it.

## Request/input

Make a one-minute CampusCart explainer from the concept PRD.

## Resolved brief

Students; English narration; 16:9; illustrated campus scenes; concept status; no claims of deployed usage.

## Decisions and expected production

Open on an unused camera, show a student finding it, agreeing a handoff and returning it, then close on the benefit. Use a consistent camera/people throughout. Produce a voice audition, time scenes from speech, show a brief pilot, then render with captions. The repo includes a script/storyboard and a separately labeled silent motion demonstration.

## Questions to avoid

Do not ask again for the stated audience, language, direction or output. Do not ask the user to choose minor spacing or filenames. Ask about a material missing fact/capability if it changes completion.

## What would fail

Invented product evidence, generic interchangeable treatment, an unrun check described as passed, or a production plan presented as a completed artifact. Apply the task-specific acceptance checks, not just this summary.
