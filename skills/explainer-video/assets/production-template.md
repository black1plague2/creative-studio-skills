## Filled example — OpenBlock report demo

Domain: [civic/nonprofit](../../../domains/civic-nonprofit.md). Fictional reporting prototype; no municipal integration or repair guarantee. Audience: adult residents. Planning target: approximately 35–50 seconds, 16:9; final timing follows an actually recorded/listened voice.

| Scene | Spoken draft | Visual action |
|---|---|---|
| Issue | “A damaged footpath is easier to explain when the problem is clear.” | Generic neighborhood scene; highlight the damaged patch, not a suffering-person montage |
| Describe | “In this demo, choose the issue and describe its approximate location.” | Illustrated choice/description action, no private address |
| Receipt | “The receipt shows that the report was received. It does not mean a repair has been scheduled.” | Receipt changes visibly to demo status; do not animate a promised repair crew |
| Close | “Make the issue clear. This is a fictional reporting prototype.” | Return to neighborhood scene with exact status |

Pilot risk: narration/receipt must distinguish received versus scheduled. Voice direction: one helpful neighbor explaining a task, warm but factual; no exaggerated uplifting ending. Screen label remains “Fictional reporting prototype.” No scene duration is asserted as measured yet.

## Review and handoff

Listen to a real audition and pilot; inspect scene fragments before full render. Check final audio alone, then image/audio together: pronunciation, unwanted words, joins, clipping, caption timing, readable dwell, meaningful movement and scene continuity. Inspect real frames and final playback plus export metadata. A still-image montage is not assumed to satisfy a moving demonstration.
