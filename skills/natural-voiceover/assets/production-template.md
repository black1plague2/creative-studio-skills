## Filled example — FernStay concept narration

Domain: [hospitality](../../../domains/hospitality.md). Original fictional guesthouse, no real property/facility claims. Draft:

> “Imagine a quieter weekend. A courtyard with ferns. A corner for a book. A room where the day can slow down. FernStay is a guesthouse concept, not an operating property. Explore the idea, one space at a time.”

Delivery: an adult host speaking to one guest; warm and unhurried without elongated luxury-ad pacing. Keep the status sentence as clear as the atmospheric opening. “FernStay” is an invented name; exercise pronunciation assumption: “fern stay,” recorded explicitly and revised if the user supplies another pronunciation. Do not speak unsupported “accessible rooms,” “five-star” or “available this weekend” phrases.

Audition uses the first two sentences and the status sentence so warmth does not hide clarity. Record a human take if that is the user's desired voice; synthesis is optional and subject to actual capability/rights. Timing is pending until audio exists.

## Listening gate

First listen without picture: understand meaning, connected phrasing, correct terms, plausible pauses, no repetition/unwanted speech/clipped ending/jarring joins. Then listen with picture: anchors align and music does not obscure speech. Inspect final audio after stitching/mixing, not only individual takes. Loudness target follows actual destination, not a universal number.
