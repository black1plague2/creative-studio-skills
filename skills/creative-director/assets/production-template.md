## Filled example — VisitBridge concept bundle

Domain: [healthtech](../../../domains/healthtech.md). Source: the pack's fictional scheduling brief. Audience: first-time adult visitors; action: understand that a received scheduling request is not a confirmed visit. Requested bundle: three GPT 16:9 poster slides, one visitor PDF and a 45-second planning target for a narrated explainer. No clinical content or improvement data exists.

Shared direction: calm editorial visitor/calendar/receipt scenes with clear dark copy on a warm field. Slides are complete GPT-generated images, not template text boxes. PDF is a reading document with semantic text. Video is a scene sequence tied to measured narration, not an automatic slide montage.

| Claim | Source status | Use and qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Request receipt follows slot selection | Proposed | All artifacts; “fictional scheduling prototype” |
| Staff confirmation happens in prototype | Unsupported | Exclude; explain that confirmation is outside scope |
| Clinical outcomes improve | No evidence | Exclude |

Capability decision for a text-only host: complete three clearly labeled handoffs; actual images, rendered PDF, audio listening and final video remain pending. One major risk: receipt artwork could imply confirmation; inspect copy and scene cues together. Material questions: none for these concept handoffs; real clinic identity/source/booking terms would be required before a real service artifact.
