## Filled example — LanternLab practice workshop

Domain: [education](../../../domains/education.md). Fictional adult digital-literacy exercise. No real event date, registration page, accreditation or learning-outcome evidence exists. Produce design drafts, not a public event campaign.

Variants: (1) square concept card showing message inspection, headline “Pause. Check the sender. Inspect the link.”; (2) three-page carousel: inspect sender → inspect destination → practice with feedback; (3) tall concept card that emphasizes the learner's next action. All retain “Fictional learning activity” in visible copy. CTA: “Explore the practice concept.” No invented sign-up link, “certified course” badge or school partnership logo.

Visual identity: warm desktop/message editorial illustration, dark readable type and consistent annotation color. Example dimensions remain planning targets until chosen platform specs are checked. The carousel's last card gives a useful next thought, not a filler thank-you page.
