# Social campaigns: production rules

These rules apply to the selected task and supplied brief. Use judgment for reversible choices; preserve explicit user requirements.

## 1. Extract the real offer and audience decision

Extract the real offer and audience decision. Do not invent discounts, deadlines, customers, performance claims or endorsements to make an ad more persuasive.

## 2. Check current primary platform format guidance when exact specifications matter

Check current primary platform format guidance when exact specifications matter. A remembered crop/safe-area rule can be outdated; make adaptations from the actual placement.

## 3. Create a shared visual/copy direction, then give variants a meaningful difference: benefit emphasis, audience context, image treatment or CTA framing

Create a shared visual/copy direction, then give variants a meaningful difference: benefit emphasis, audience context, image treatment or CTA framing. Color swaps alone are weak experiments.

## 4. Design for the available attention and viewport

Design for the available attention and viewport. A carousel needs a useful progression; a thumbnail needs a clear recognizable subject; a small feed image cannot carry a whole dense report.

## 5. Use original or cleared visuals, truthful product screens and exact brand/offer copy

Use original or cleared visuals, truthful product screens and exact brand/offer copy. Label conceptual product imagery. Preserve dates, terms and disclosures appropriate to the actual claim.

## 6. Keep text safe from platform chrome/crops and inspect actual export dimensions

Keep text safe from platform chrome/crops and inspect actual export dimensions. Adapt composition instead of squeezing the same rectangle into every aspect ratio.

## 7. Write precise copy with one useful action

Write precise copy with one useful action. Avoid unearned hype and fake urgency. Distinguish a draft ad concept from approved publication copy.

## 8. Provide alt descriptions/text equivalents where needed

Provide alt descriptions/text equivalents where needed. Check contrast and readability on a representative phone-sized preview and in each chosen language.

## 9. Deliver a named export set, copy sheet, variant intent and any real placement limitations

Deliver a named export set, copy sheet, variant intent and any real placement limitations. Do not publish, schedule or message it unless the user explicitly authorizes that action.

## 10. For campaign measurement plans, identify a real comparison and success measure without promising engagement/conversion results

For campaign measurement plans, identify a real comparison and success measure without promising engagement/conversion results. Document assumptions as hypotheses.
