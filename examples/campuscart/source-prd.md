# CampusCart — fictional concept PRD

## Status and purpose

This is an original fictional exercise for a creative-skills repository. No product is claimed to be launched. No adoption, revenue, survey, performance or environmental-impact data is supplied.

## Audience and problem hypothesis

Adult university students sometimes need an item briefly while another student owns an item that is not currently being used. Cameras, books and bicycles are example categories. The hypothesis is illustrative and has not been validated by a survey.

## Concept

CampusCart is a proposed campus item-borrowing app. Its core flow is:

1. Find an item listing.
2. Agree a handoff with its owner.
3. Return the item.

## Proposed requirements

- Listings describe the item and availability.
- A borrowing request includes intended dates.
- Both parties agree a handoff and a return.
- Unavailable items and invalid dates have clear feedback.

## Out of scope

No payment, insurance, identity verification, backend deployment, external messaging or real customer claims are implemented by this exercise.
