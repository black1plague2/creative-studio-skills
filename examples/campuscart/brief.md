# Resolved brief

- Deliverable: three complete GPT-generated landscape poster-slide images, PDF, image-based PPTX, prompt/copy records.
- Audience: students and hackathon-style judges.
- Objective: explain a fictional campus borrowing idea and its primary flow.
- Language: English.
- Sequence: concept cover, illustrated problem, illustrated three-stage workflow.
- Direction: original campus world, hand-drawn contours, warm paper/cut-paper texture, expressive display lettering.
- Palette: forest green, cream, marigold and sky blue.
- Continuity: recurring adult student characters, a camera and campus buildings.
- Density: open cover/problem; a content-bearing workflow with exact stage labels.
- Evidence: fictional concept; no invented product statistics or deployed-feature claims.
- Generation: built-in GPT image tool; prompts saved as source records.
- Output geometry: fixed 16:9; native integer dimension rounding disclosed, no stretch/crop.

No user intake questions are necessary for this resolved exercise. A live project with an unknown audience, language, fixed count or visual direction should ask those material questions first.
