# Voice audition and listening plan

No voice is included or approved in this example.

Use this representative line: “Then, agree a handoff with its owner. Choose when you need it and when you will bring it back.”

Resolve language/accent/narrator with the user if unknown. Record or synthesize the line with natural phrasing; avoid equal emphasis on every clause. Verify “CampusCart,” “handoff,” numbers/dates if introduced, and the final consonants.

Listen to the audition without the picture: understandable meaning, conversational rhythm, useful pauses, appropriate tone, no repetition/unwanted words/clipped endings. Then listen with the intended scene: the action should occur at the spoken anchor, captions should be readable, and joins should preserve continuity.

Measure the audio and update storyboard timing. Rewrite redundant script before accelerating speech. If direct listening is unavailable, provide the audio and state that a user audition remains; do not treat a waveform, transcript or preset name as approval.
