# CampusCart concept explainer: spoken-script draft

Fictional concept. Planning target: approximately 45–60 seconds; actual recorded/generated narration must determine timing. Tone: conversational, specific and calm. Language: English for this example; a real project should resolve accent/narrator preference.

> A camera on your shelf. A camera someone needs for the afternoon.
>
> CampusCart is a concept for borrowing things within a campus community.
>
> First, find an item and check its availability.
>
> Then, agree a handoff with its owner. Choose when you need it and when you will bring it back.
>
> Use it for the day. Return it as agreed.
>
> If an item is unavailable, choose another. If the dates do not work, correct them before requesting it.
>
> Borrow what you need. Share what you own.
