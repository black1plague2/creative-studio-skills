# Hospitality: vivid atmosphere with accurate practical detail

Use for accommodation, dining, experiences, guest guides and venue creatives. Atmosphere helps decision-making, but location, room/facility claims, accessibility, availability and booking terms must be real when presented as real.

## Editable brief

- Venue/experience, guest segment and intended booking/enquiry action: [fill].
- Real photography versus concept rendering: [fill].
- Location, facilities, access details and operating information: [fill from source].
- Rates, dates, taxes, cancellation and availability: [fill if applicable].
- Language, viewing channel and print/use context: [fill].
- Which services exist and which are only proposed: [fill].

Ask: Which facilities and access details are verified? Is the CTA an enquiry or a confirmed booking? Which dates, prices and conditions can be shown? Do not invent ratings, distance-to-attraction claims, allergen guarantees or accessibility certifications.

## Creative constraints

Use intentional editorial pacing, real or clearly illustrative spaces and readable practical copy. Avoid presenting a generated room as documentary photography. Do not infer step-free access from a room image. Website availability, booking request and confirmed reservation are distinct states; the final receipt language must match actual integration.

## Filled fictional exercise — FernStay

Original guesthouse concept for adult weekend travelers. Illustration brief: a small fern-lined courtyard, reading nook and room entrance. These are imagined spaces, not actual facilities. Proposed site offers a concept gallery and an enquiry form; no inventory, rate, date, address, cancellation policy or payment system is supplied. Exact copy: **“A quieter weekend, imagined.”** CTA: **“Explore the guesthouse concept.”** Image caption: **“Illustrative concept; not an operating property.”**

## Task adaptations

| Group | Domain-specific treatment |
|---|---|
| Brief and language | Preserve imagined/operating distinction; do not turn an enquiry into booking confirmation. |
| Slide and poster | Complete GPT 16:9 scene posters communicate courtyard-to-reading-nook atmosphere with intentional large copy; no fake review-stars or rates. |
| Reading documents | Concept booklet combines clear spatial captions and an explicit unspecified-facilities list; a real guest guide needs verified times, contacts and access information. |
| Website and app | Gallery navigation works; enquiry status states what happened. Real booking UI needs selected dates, unavailable inventory, price detail and actual confirmation semantics. |
| Video, voice and interaction | Narration is warm and measured rather than an exaggerated luxury advertisement; listening gate checks breath/pacing. Interactive walkthrough reveals spaces with truthful concept labels. |
| Identity, assets and campaign | Restrained botanical/editorial identity; asset list marks each rendering illustrative. Social crops preserve facility/status qualifications. |
| Data and evidence | No occupancy, rating or distance comparison without sources. Concept room counts cannot imply available inventory. |

Review failure example: a caption says “Step-free rooms available” because the entrance illustration has no stairs. Ask for verified access information and remove the claim meanwhile.
